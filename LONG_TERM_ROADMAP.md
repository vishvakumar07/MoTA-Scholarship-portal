## 2. Invariant "No Feature Creep" Boundary

The platform explicitly rejects the following unneeded features unless mandated by statutory policy:
- **NO BLOCKCHAIN:** Relational PostgreSQL with immutable append-only audit tables and cryptographic hashing provides superior defensibility and lower operational cost.
- **NO PREDICTIVE RISK SCORING:** Public welfare scholarships cannot employ algorithmic risk profiling that could penalize disadvantaged tribal students.
- **NO AUTOMATED AI APPROVAL/REJECTION:** Human constitutional accountability must govern all sanction decisions.
