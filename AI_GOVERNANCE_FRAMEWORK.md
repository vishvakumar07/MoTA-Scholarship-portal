# Ministry of Tribal Affairs (MoTA) Scholarship & Fellowship Management System
# Long-Term AI Ethics, Governance & Model Safety Framework

> **Classification:** Sovereign AI Governance Framework  
> **Status:** RATIFIED POLICY (Phase 16)  
> **Core Invariant:** AI is Strictly Assistive; Eligibility is Strictly Deterministic

---

## 1. The Four Non-Negotiable Boundaries of AI Governance

```text
=============================================================================
  AI ENGINE                    ==>   STRICTLY ASSISTIVE
  DETERMINISTIC RULE ENGINE    ==>   SOLE ELIGIBILITY DETERMINATION AUTHORITY
  AUTHENTICATED HUMAN OFFICER  ==>   SOLE OFFICIAL ADMINISTRATIVE DECISION MAKER
  AUTHORIZED GOVERNMENT SYSTEM ==>   SOLE EXTERNAL VERIFICATION AUTHORITY
=============================================================================
```

Under no circumstances shall any future engineering modification grant an AI model, LLM, or automated algorithm the authority to:
1. Automatically approve an applicant for a scholarship.
2. Automatically reject an applicant for a scholarship.
3. Determine, alter, or interpret statutory eligibility rules.
4. Verify or attest the authenticity of government identity documents.
5. Override an authorized human scrutiny officer.

---

## 2. Model Evolution & Change Management

Any transition to newer model versions (e.g. Gemini 1.5, Claude, or sovereign Indic LLMs) requires:
1. **Model Evaluation:** Benchmarking on an authorized synthetic test set of 200+ sample certificates.
2. **Hallucination & Bias Audit:** Verifying that the model does not inject synthetic text or exhibit demographic bias.
3. **Data Minimization Verification:** Ensuring all applicant PII is stripped prior to inference.
4. **Formal Sign-off:** Approval by the AI Governance Officer and MoTA Directorate.
